How is Zarok different from a regular planner?

A promise you make to yourself

Name what matters, and Zarok helps you keep your word every day: it reminds you, checks it off and shows your streak.

Courses with stages and breaks

Set stages, active periods and pauses once. Zarok shows the right step on the right day.

Say it in your own words

By voice or text, in one sentence: AI Zarok spreads your tasks across days and hours, and you review them before saving. You can also connect your own ChatGPT, Claude or Gemini for free.

A living companion

Seven characters: Topa, Kapa, Spark, Toasty, Tune, Zorik and Lumi. Zarok cheers your progress on screen and on widgets and changes along with your day.
