App statistics

You can turn usage counters off. They don't include the content of your entries.

AI Zarok and consent

AI Zarok works once you agree that your request is sent to a third-party AI service. You can withdraw consent and clear the chat.

Profile → AI Zarok & subscription

Calendar and permissions
