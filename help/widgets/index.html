Widgets on Android

Widgets show your plan and let you check off tasks from your phone's home screen. They work in the Android app.

  1. 1

    Open “Zarok widgets”

    On the “Profile” tab at the bottom of the screen, tap “Zarok widgets” in the “For your day” group. At the top of the gallery, in the “Your Zarok” block, choose which Zarok appears on the widgets. There are seven: Topa, Kapa, Spark, Toasty, Tune, Zorik, and Lumi. Your choice applies right away. Each widget swipes separately: the dots below it show its states.

  2. 2

    Add a widget with the button

    Find the widget you want and tap “＋ Add widget.” Confirm in the phone's dialog. The “Today” widget (“Zarok · day” in the phone's widget list) shows the whole day: the morning ritual, prep, and tasks by time, each with a checkmark.

  3. 3

    Add it by hand if there's no dialog

    Press and hold an empty spot on the home screen, choose “Widgets,” then “Zarok.” Find the widget you want, press and hold it, and drag it into place. This path depends on your phone and launcher. The in-app button adds the widget by itself only on Android 8.0 and newer, and only if the launcher supports it; in other cases, the app will show you this path.

  4. 4

    Resize it

    Drag the widget's frame to make it bigger or smaller. Your phone handles this, so the frame looks different depending on the launcher. All Zarok widgets can be resized wider and taller. “Day” is 4 × 2 by default. A small “Day” shows the count in a ring and a strip; a tall one shows the day's items with checkmarks.

  5. 5

    Move widgets and tasks

    To move a widget on the screen, drag it. Zarok widgets don't have “up” and “down” buttons. Tasks inside a widget are sorted by time: earlier ones go higher, and tasks without a time go at the end. To move a task up, open it in “Day” and set an earlier time.

  6. 6

    Check off a task

    Tap the task's row: the checkmark appears right away. Tapping again removes the check. In some widget views, only the checkmark next to the task checks it off, and tapping the name opens the app. Check-offs are saved in the widget's queue and reach the app when you open it or go back to the screen.

  7. 7

    Open the app from a widget

    Tap the widget's background or name, and the app opens to today. On the “Calendar” widget, tapping a day opens that day. On the Quit vow widget, it opens the “Vows” tab; on the AI Zarok widget, it opens the chat.

  8. 8

    Talk to AI Zarok

    The AI Zarok widget works with a “Zarok Plus” subscription. Before your first conversation, you need to confirm your age and consent to requests being processed on the AI Zarok screen. Tap the card to open the chat. Tap an example tile to put that example into the input field without sending it. Tap the microphone to start recording your voice; a second tap is “Stop,” and the text appears in the field. You can also write in your own words, for example: “Add 10 minutes of breathing to my morning ritual at 07:00” (ritual step), “Breakfast at 08:30” (meal), “Workout on Mondays and Thursdays at 19:00, 60 minutes” (event), “I'm starting a 15-day course, every day at 09:00” (course). You send the message yourself.

  9. 9

    Confirm the entry

    AI Zarok doesn't save anything on its own. Instead, a plan card appears. It has lines like “new entry · course,” “new entry · meal,” “new entry · step,” or “event change.” If a similar entry already exists, a warning appears: “A similar entry already exists.” “Apply” saves the plan, and “No thanks” cancels it. If there are several entries, the button shows how many are checked: “Apply (N).” If the plan will replace existing data, tick “I confirm replacing existing data”: without that box, “Apply” isn't available. If AI Zarok needs more details, it asks questions in a list below the message. Answer them in one message, by text or voice.

  10. 10

    Look, color, and Zarok

    In “Zarok widgets,” open “Widget style”: “Painted” puts Zarok in painted scenes, free; “Minimal” has no paintings; “Mix” shows a painting on one day and a minimal look on another. “Minimal” and “Mix” are part of the subscription. “Widget look” sets the color for all widgets: “Same as the app” is free, and the other palettes require a subscription. The “Zarok on widgets” and “Zarok's reactions” switches are free: with Zarok off on widgets, the count and tasks stay.

  11. 11

    Paid widgets and the lock

    These are part of “Zarok Plus”: “Progress,” “At your pace” (in the gallery, “Soft start”), “Quiet recap” (in the gallery, “Day recap”), “Calendar,” “My steps and dates,” “Upcoming,” “Habits,” “Focus totals,” “Moon,” and AI Zarok. “My Zarok,” “Next step,” “New task,” and the main day widgets are free. You can add a paid widget without a subscription, but instead of its content, the screen shows a lock. Tap the lock: the app offers a subscription or opening the widget with leaves (1 leaf for 1 day). Subscriptions are set up through Google Play.

  12. 12

    Set up “My steps and dates”

    When you add this widget, the setup opens; on Android 12 and newer, you can skip it. Choose a mode: “One task,” “Task · week,” “Task · history,” “Four steps,” “Key date,” or “Calendar & plan.” The gear on the widget itself opens the setup again.

  13. 13

    New widgets and task filters

    “New task” is a button on your home screen: one tap opens a new task. “Upcoming” shows tasks for today and the next six days, “Habits” shows how your habits are going over a week or a month, “Focus totals” shows the time logged with the “Focus time” timer, and “Moon” shows the current moon phase and the next full or new moon. In the “Day,” “Today,” “Schedule,” and “Upcoming” widgets, you can keep only the tasks from one folder, category, or course, or with one tag: this filter is part of Plus. With the “Hanami” and “Memes” themes, widgets take on their look.

Good to know

  • Widgets work only in the Android app.
  • If the widget shows old data, open the app: it will send the widget fresh data.
  • To undo an accidental check-off, tap the task again.
  • A lock on a widget means a paid widget without a subscription. It's not a bug.
  • Samples in the gallery show Zarok even when the switch is off, but on the widget itself, with Zarok off, there are no Zarok figures or words.
  • The “Focus time” timer is a separate widget. Add it from the “Focus time” screen (the “Day” menu) when no session is running.
Questions about this section

A little more clarity.

Why is there a lock on the widget?

That's how a paid widget appears when you don't have a “Zarok Plus” subscription. For the full list of paid widgets, see the “Paid widgets and the lock” step. The button on the lock depends on whether you've had a subscription before: “Renew,” “Try it free” (if the store offers a free trial), or “Get Plus.” Tapping it opens the subscription screen.

Why didn't the “＋ Add widget” button add anything?

The app adds the widget by itself only on Android 8.0 and newer, and only if the launcher supports it. Otherwise, a hint appears: press and hold an empty spot on the home screen, choose “Widgets” and “Zarok,” then drag the widget you want into place.

The widget shows old data. What should I do?

Open the app: when it starts, it sends the widget fresh data. After each change in the app, the widget updates on its own, almost right away.

Will I get a reminder for a task I've already checked off on the widget?

No. Checking off a task on the widget cancels its reminder.

Where do check-offs made on the widget go?

They're saved on the phone in the widget's queue and reach the app when you open it or go back to the screen. If the app is already open, this happens right away. After that, they're recorded as regular check-offs.

How is the “Progress” widget different from the “Progress” section?

The “Progress” widget (part of “Zarok Plus”) shows the day's count: how much is done and how much is left. The “Progress” section shows your streak, closed days, and time by category. By default, the section isn't in the tab bar: you can add it to the bar, or open it from “Profile,” in the “More sections” area.

Can I open a paid widget without a subscription?

Yes, for a while: with leaves, which you get for watching ads in the free Android version. 1 leaf opens a widget for 1 day, and the days add up. The AI Zarok widget can’t be opened this way. See “Ads and privacy options” for details.
