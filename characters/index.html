FORM I / VIISteadiness
First chakra · Muladhara

Topa

For when you want something simple to lean on.

“Start with one step.”

Topa draws on Muladhara, the root chakra. We turned the idea of steadiness into a calm elephant calf: it stands firmly on the ground and never rushes anyone. In Zarok's world, it stands for small, repeatable actions that add up to a reliable day.

  • Calm at the start of the day
  • Respect for small steps
  • Warm support after a pause

THE STORY OF THE COLLECTION

From anchor
to meaning.

The seven chakras became the starting point for an artistic collection. We took their motifs (steadiness, movement, will, care, expression, focus and wholeness) and told their stories through seven living characters.

Zaroks accompany your routine in illustrations and widgets: they celebrate your check-offs, rest and welcome you back. Different figures help you find a presence style that feels close to you.
