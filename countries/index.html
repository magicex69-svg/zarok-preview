Where Zarok is available

Zarok is offered in the countries and regions where Google Play distributes it, except Russia and Belarus.

Countries and regions where Google Play is available ↗

Russia and Belarus

In Russia and Belarus we do not sell the Zarok Plus subscription, do not provide support and do not offer the service. We plan to remove the app from Google Play in these countries.

Anyone who uses the app from Russia or Belarus does so on their own initiative and is responsible for complying with local law.
