The bot @day_calendar_bot helps you keep track of your tasks, share them, and open the app right inside Telegram.

Open the bot Stickers and emoji

How to connect

  1. Open the bot @day_calendar_bot in Telegram.

  2. In the Zarok app, connect Telegram to your Zarok. Your entries will be the same in both.

  3. If you already had entries in Telegram, choose: move them over or start fresh.

  4. To sign in from another device, use a code.

What the bot can do

Menu under the input field

Open any section with the buttons under the input field.

Day summary

Today’s tasks in a table with check marks. You can pin it to the top of the chat.

Week by day

All the tasks of the week, shown in tables by day.

AI Zarok on subscription

Describe what to change in your own words, and AI Zarok suggests a plan. Available with the Zarok Plus subscription.

Assign a task

Pick a list, a person and a date, or write it in one phrase, for example “assign Anna to buy bread tomorrow”.

Scheduled sending

The task reaches the person at the set time. The Scheduled section lists them in a table.

Mini app

Zarok opens full screen right inside Telegram, like a regular app.

Support

Write to support right from the bot. The reply comes back there.

Quickly from any chat

In any chat, type @day_calendar_bot and a task, for example: “@day_calendar_bot buy cat food tomorrow 10:00”. A task card with an “Add to my Zarok” button goes into the chat. You can also type “@day_calendar_bot today” to share your tasks for today.

Tasks together in a group

The group has a shared task list. Tap “Take it” and the task becomes yours. You can assign a task right from the group, and the chat owner manages the chat settings and language.

FAQ

Do I need a subscription to use the bot?+

Tasks, summaries, groups and the mini app work without it. AI Zarok is available with the Zarok Plus subscription.

What happens to my entries from Telegram?+

If you already had entries in Telegram, the connection asks you to choose: move them over or start fresh.

Can I use it on another phone?+

Yes. Signing in on another device is done with a code.

Which languages does the bot speak?+

You choose: Russian, English or Ukrainian.

What if something does not work?+

Write to support right from the bot. The reply comes back there.
