  1. Set the phases

    Each phase has a name and a number of days. A phase is either active or a break.

  2. Repeat the cycle

    The counter repeats all phases in a row, 1 to 24 times. You see the full length of the course right away.

  3. Pick a time

    You choose the time of the item in your day. Turn on a reminder with the Remind me checkbox.

  4. Save

    The course goes into your calendar for the whole term at once.

Habits and the morning ritual

  • A habit is made of steps, each with its own time, length and number of times a day.
  • A ritual step shows up first on the Morning screen of a new day.
  • You can add things to prepare in advance and a video link to a step.
  • On break days the steps are hidden.

Quits: cut back step by step

  • Choose what you are cutting back on, the unit, how much you have a day now and a term of 14 to 90 days.
  • Zarok works out the daily limit and shows when it reaches zero.
  • The Urge button opens a three-minute timer, and Ritual opens the short steps of Your moment.
  • If a day goes over the limit, you can keep the plan, shift day 0 or go gentler.

Zarok is a diary and a planner. It gives no medical advice and promises no results.

Questions about courses and habits

Are courses, habits and quits free?+

Yes. Creating and keeping courses, habits, rituals and quits is free. Zarok Plus adds AI Zarok and extra widgets.

How is a course different from a habit?+

A course has phases and breaks with one item in your day. A habit is made of steps, each with its own time; it also runs by day from the start date, except on break days.

Can I repeat a course after it ends?+

Yes. On the Day tab, swipe a course item right, tap Repeat and pick the date the copy starts. Check-offs do not carry over to the copy.

Can I manage courses from ChatGPT or Claude?+

Yes. Connect them to Zarok through MCP using the guide on the Connections page: a connected AI app reads your plan and, if you allow it, changes it.

Is this a medical app?+

No. Zarok is a calendar and planner. You set the names, numbers and terms yourself.
