Zarok for Claude and ChatGPT

Connect Zarok to Claude or ChatGPT and work with your daily plan right in their chat: ask what is on tomorrow, add a task, move a meeting, or check off what is done. The client can change your plan only if you allow it, and every change can be undone in Zarok within a day.

What does the connector do?

Zarok runs an MCP server (Model Context Protocol, an open way to connect outside services to AI apps). Claude and ChatGPT call it on your behalf after you sign in to your Zarok account and approve the connection. You decide what the client may do: only read, or also change your plan.

How to connect

  1. 1

    You need an account

    Connecting is free, no subscription needed. You need a Zarok account with sync turned on.

  2. 2

    Add the address in the client

    Pick your client below and add Zarok with the server address. The exact menu path is in the client tab. Availability depends on your plan with the client.

  3. 3

    Approve the sign-in

    The client opens the Zarok connection page. Sign in and press “Connect”.

  4. 4

    Choose permissions

    By default the client can only read. If you turn on “Allow plan changes from chat”, it can change your plan itself or send you changes to approve.

Signing in on the connection page

When the client opens the Zarok connection page, you can sign in to your account right there: with your email and password, or with a code from an email. Then choose the permissions and press “Connect”. You do not need your phone. If you have no email sign-in, type the code from the page into the app: Profile → “Settings” → “Connections” → “AI clients”.

What it can do, in plain words

The tools fall into reading, changing and proposing. Which ones a client can use depends on the permissions you grant when you connect.

Read (read permission)

  • Day plan

    Tasks, events, course steps and meals for a chosen date, with the done mark.

  • Week summary

    How many entries are planned and checked off across seven days. Counts only.

  • Period statistics

    Up to 185 days: totals, weekly trend, weekdays, time of day, categories, streaks. It counts check-offs and is not a health assessment.

  • Habits and rituals

    Your habits and their steps.

  • Meals of a day

    The meals of a chosen date: time, items, portions and repeat rule.

  • Courses with doses

    Your courses: dose times, intake and break phases, dose text, start and end dates.

  • Companion status

    Which Zarok character you chose. Read only, with no rewards or leaves.

  • Together lists

    Your shared lists and their items (title, note, date, priority, done). Other members’ names, comments, files and secret tasks are not shown. Available when shared lists are switched on in Zarok.

Technical tool names
  • zarok_get_plan Day plan
  • zarok_get_week_summary Week summary
  • zarok_get_period_stats Period statistics
  • zarok_get_habits Habits and rituals
  • zarok_get_meals Meals of a day
  • zarok_get_courses Courses with doses
  • zarok_get_companion Companion status
  • zarok_get_shared_lists Together lists

Change the plan (only if you allow it)

  • Apply changes

    Add a task, event or habit; move a task; change its title, time, length or note; remove a task for one day; check off and uncheck; set priority and tags. Courses: add, change phases and doses, stop from a date. Meals: add, change, remove a meal. Delete a whole habit. Up to 20 actions at once, all or nothing.

  • Add to a shared list

    A new item in a Together list, if you are an editor or owner there. Other members see it as yours.

  • Check off in a shared list

    Mark an item done, or undo that.

Technical tool names
  • zarok_apply_changes Apply changes
  • zarok_add_shared_list_item Add to a shared list
  • zarok_set_shared_list_item_done Check off in a shared list

Propose (comes with plan changes)

  • Propose changes

    The client drafts changes, but your plan stays as it is until you approve them in Zarok.

  • Proposal status

    Approved, dismissed or waiting for your decision.

Technical tool names
  • zarok_propose_changes Propose changes
  • zarok_get_proposal_status Proposal status

Every change to the plan is recorded: in Zarok (Profile → “Settings” → “Connections” → “AI clients”, the Changes tab) you can undo it within 24 hours, all at once or one by one. Your own later edits are not overwritten. Items and check-offs in shared lists are not part of this log: remove them in the list itself.

Example requests

  • What is on my plan for tomorrow?
  • Move my reading to Friday at 9 pm.
  • Add a 20-minute walk every day at 6 pm.
  • Show which meals I have on Thursday.
  • Add “Buy bread” to our Together list and check off the milk.
  • How many days this month did I check off reading?

Data and privacy

  • The client receives only what your request needs and the permissions you granted allow: the plan of chosen days, habits, courses, meals and check-off counts. Your Zarok password and account keys are never handed over.
  • Shared lists are read for you only: names and ids of other members, comments, attachments and invitations are not returned, and secret tasks are hidden.
  • Data goes to the service you connected yourself (Claude or ChatGPT). From there it is handled under that service’s own rules and privacy policy.
  • You can revoke access at any time: Profile → “Settings” → “Connections” → “AI clients” → “Access” → “Revoke access”. Connections are erased when the account is deleted.
  • The history of a client’s changes is kept for 30 days and undo data for 24 hours. Access keys are stored only as fingerprints.
  • The connection always uses encrypted HTTPS. We do not sell your data or use it for advertising.
  • Zarok gives no medical advice: course and dose data are records you entered yourself.
