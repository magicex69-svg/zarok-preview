CONNECTOR FOR CLAUDE AND CHATGPT

Zarok for Claude and ChatGPT

Connect Zarok to Claude or ChatGPT and your daily plan is available in their chat: ask what is on for tomorrow, add a task, move a meeting, check off what is done. The client can change your plan only if you allow it, and every change can be undone in Zarok within a day.

Step-by-step connection guide

What the connector does

Zarok runs an MCP server (Model Context Protocol, an open way to connect outside services to AI apps). Claude and ChatGPT call it on your behalf after you sign in to your Zarok account and approve the connection. You decide what the client may do: only read, or also change your plan.

Server address

https://zarok.app/mcp

Streamable HTTP, sign-in with OAuth and approval in your Zarok account.

Connect step by step: choose your client

Claude

The Claude app and website.

  1. Open the connector settings (button below): Customize, Connectors.

  2. Select “+ Add”, then “Add custom connector”.

  3. Name: Zarok. Server address: copy it with the button above and paste it. Select “Continue”, then “Add”.

  4. Select “Connect”: the Zarok page opens. Sign in there and press “Connect”.

Open Claude settings

Claude Code
claude mcp add --transport http zarok https://zarok.app/mcp

ChatGPT

The ChatGPT web version on a computer. Availability depends on your plan.

  1. Open ChatGPT on a computer: Settings, Apps, Advanced settings. Turn on Developer mode.

  2. Select “Create”. Name: Zarok. Paste the server address (button above) into the MCP server URL field.

  3. Choose OAuth for sign-in, confirm that you trust it, and select “Create”.

  4. The Zarok page opens. Sign in there and press “Connect”.

Open ChatGPT

Codex
codex mcp add zarok --url https://zarok.app/mcp

Gemini CLI

For those who have Gemini CLI installed on a computer.

  1. Run the command in a terminal.

  2. Start gemini and enter /mcp auth zarok.

  3. The Zarok page opens in your browser. Sign in there and press “Connect”.

Command
gemini mcp add --transport http zarok https://zarok.app/mcp

Another client

Any client that supports MCP.

  1. It needs a remote MCP server (Streamable HTTP) and OAuth sign-in with PKCE.

  2. Paste the server address into its settings and sign in on the Zarok page.

Signing in on the connection page

When the client opens the Zarok connection page, you can sign in to your account right there: with your email and password, or with a code from an email. Then choose the permissions and press “Connect”. You do not need your phone. If you have no email sign-in, type the code from the page into the app: Profile, Integrations and import, Connect an AI app.

What it can do, in plain words

The tools fall into reading, changing and proposing. Which ones a client can use depends on the permissions you grant when you connect.

Read (read permission)

  • Day plan zarok_get_plan

    Tasks, events, course steps and meals for a chosen date, with the done mark.

  • Week summary zarok_get_week_summary

    How many entries are planned and checked off across seven days. Counts only.

  • Period statistics zarok_get_period_stats

    Up to 185 days: totals, weekly trend, weekdays, time of day, categories, streaks. It counts check-offs and is not a health assessment.

  • Habits and rituals zarok_get_habits

    Your habits and their steps.

  • Meals of a day zarok_get_meals

    The meals of a chosen date: time, items, portions and repeat rule.

  • Courses with doses zarok_get_courses

    Your courses: dose times, intake and break phases, dose text, start and end dates.

  • Companion status zarok_get_companion

    Which Zarok character you chose. It never awards anything.

  • Together lists zarok_get_shared_lists

    Your shared lists and their items (title, note, date, priority, done). Other members’ names, comments, files and secret tasks are not shown. Available when the service operator has switched this on.

Change the plan (only if you allow it)

  • Apply changes zarok_apply_changes

    Add a task, event or habit; move a task; change its title, time, length or note; remove a task for one day; check off and uncheck; set priority and tags. Courses: add, change phases and doses, stop from a date. Meals: add, change, remove a meal. Delete a whole habit. Up to 20 actions at once, all or nothing.

  • Add to a shared list zarok_add_shared_list_item

    A new item in a Together list, if you are an editor or owner there. Other members see it as yours.

  • Check off in a shared list zarok_set_shared_list_item_done

    Mark an item done, or undo that.

Propose (propose permission)

  • Propose changes zarok_propose_changes

    The client drafts changes, but your plan stays as it is until you approve them in Zarok.

  • Proposal status zarok_get_proposal_status

    Approved, dismissed or waiting for your decision.

Every change to the plan is recorded: in Zarok (Profile, Integrations and import, the Changes tab) you can undo it within 24 hours, all at once or one by one. Your own later edits are not overwritten. Items and check-offs in shared lists are not part of this log: remove them in the list itself.

Example requests

  • What is on my plan for tomorrow?
  • Move my reading to Friday at 9 pm.
  • Add a 20-minute walk every day at 6 pm.
  • Show which meals I have on Thursday.
  • Add “Buy bread” to our Together list and check off the milk.
  • How many days this month did I check off reading?

How to connect

  1. You need an account

    Connecting is free, no subscription needed. You need a Zarok account with sync turned on.

  2. Add the address in the client

    Pick your client below and add Zarok with the address https://zarok.app/mcp. In Claude it is Customize, Connectors, Add custom connector. In ChatGPT it is Settings, Apps, Developer mode, Create, on a computer. Availability depends on your plan with the client.

  3. Approve the sign-in

    The client opens the Zarok connection page. Sign in to your Zarok account and press “Connect”. Or type the code from that page into the app: Profile, Integrations and import, Connect an AI app.

  4. Choose permissions

    By default the client can only read. It can change your plan if you turn on “Allow plan changes from chat”.

Data and privacy

  • The client receives only what your request needs and the permissions you granted allow: the plan of chosen days, habits, courses, meals and check-off counts. Your Zarok password and account keys are never handed over.
  • Shared lists are read for you only: names and ids of other members, comments, attachments and invitations are not returned, and secret tasks are hidden.
  • Data goes to the service you connected yourself (Claude or ChatGPT). From there it is handled under that service’s own rules and privacy policy.
  • You can revoke access at any time: Profile, Integrations and import, Connect an AI app, Access, Revoke access. Connections are erased when the account is deleted.
  • The history of a client’s changes is kept for 30 days and undo data for 24 hours. Access keys are stored only as fingerprints.
  • Zarok gives no medical advice: course and dose data are records you entered yourself.
