Google Calendar and Tasks

Tasks and events from the Zarok list you choose appear in Google Calendar and Google Tasks. Changes made there come back to Zarok.

Open the app

What it does

  • A Zarok list (Work, Home or any of yours) is linked to a Google calendar or task list.
  • Tasks and one-time events go to the service. Edits and deletions there come back to Zarok.
  • One Google sign-in opens both the calendar and the tasks.

How to connect

  1. 1

    Open Work services

    In Zarok, tap Profile, then Integrations and import, then Work services. You need a Zarok account. Connecting is optional, and you turn it on yourself.

  2. 2

    Tap Connect

    Tap Connect next to Google Calendar and Tasks. The sign-in page opens in your browser.

  3. 3

    Sign in to Google

    Choose your Google account and allow Zarok to work with your calendar and tasks. One sign-in covers both services.

  4. 4

    Choose what goes where

    For each Zarok list, pick a Google calendar or task list, then the direction: both ways, only from Zarok, or only into Zarok.

  5. 5

    Check the result

    Add a task in the linked list. It appears in Google. To pull in edits right away, tap Sync now.

Changes made in Google change tasks in Zarok. You can undo them in Changes within 24 hours.

Try it after connecting

Add a task to your Work list and open Google Calendar.

Slack

Turn Slack messages into Zarok tasks with a reaction, a command or the message menu.

Open the app

What it does

  • This is not a calendar but a helper for tasks. A message becomes a task only when you act on it.
  • Zarok never posts to your channels and never reads channel history.
  • Each channel can be linked to a Zarok list. Tasks from that channel land in that list.

How to connect

  1. 1

    Open Work services

    In Zarok, tap Profile, then Integrations and import, then Work services. You need a Zarok account. Connecting is optional, and you turn it on yourself.

  2. 2

    Tap Connect

    Tap Connect next to Slack. The sign-in page opens in your browser.

  3. 3

    Sign in to Slack

    Choose a workspace and allow Zarok to be installed. Zarok connects to one Slack workspace, and only the person who connected it can add tasks.

  4. 4

    Choose a channel for the list

    For a Zarok list, pick a Slack channel such as #work. The direction here is single: from Slack into Zarok. Tasks from direct messages and unlinked channels go to the first linked list.

  5. 5

    Add a task from a message

    Three ways. React with :zarok: (or ➕, :heavy_plus_sign:). In the message menu, choose Add to Zarok. Or type the /zarok command followed by the task. In the command you can add tomorrow or a date as YYYY-MM-DD.

  6. 6

    Add Zarok to the channel

    For reactions, Zarok has to be in the channel: type /invite @Zarok there. Each Slack workspace adds the :zarok: emoji on its own, while ➕ works right away. The message menu and the command work without this.

If the morning summary is on, your task list for the day arrives in your Slack direct messages. If there are no tasks, there is no message. You can undo created tasks in Changes within 24 hours.

Try it after connecting

/zarok buy flowers tomorrow

Todoist

Todoist projects are linked to Zarok lists. Tasks, edits, completion and deletion go both ways.

Open the app

What it does

  • A Zarok list is linked to a Todoist project, and tasks become Todoist tasks.
  • Title, note, due date, completion and deletion stay in sync.
  • You choose the direction: both ways, only from Zarok, or only into Zarok.

How to connect

  1. 1

    Open Work services

    In Zarok, tap Profile, then Integrations and import, then Work services. You need a Zarok account. Connecting is optional, and you turn it on yourself.

  2. 2

    Tap Connect

    Tap Connect next to Todoist. The sign-in page opens in your browser.

  3. 3

    Sign in to Todoist

    Sign in to your Todoist account and allow Zarok to work with your tasks and projects.

  4. 4

    Choose what goes where

    For each Zarok list, pick a Todoist project (Inbox works too) and the direction: both ways, only from Zarok, or only into Zarok. Archived projects are not offered.

  5. 5

    Check the result

    Add a task in the linked list and open Todoist: the task is there. Complete it in Todoist, and the task in Zarok is checked off too.

Repeating Zarok events are not sent to Todoist, because Todoist tasks have no such series.

Try it after connecting

Complete a task in Todoist and look at the task in Zarok.
How to disconnect

How to disconnect

You can disconnect a service at any time.

  1. Open Profile, Integrations and import, Work services.
  2. Tap Disconnect next to the service.

What gets deleted

On our side: the access to the service, list bindings and task links. If the service allows it, Zarok first revokes its access there.

What stays

Tasks and events that already exist in Zarok and in the service. Disconnecting does not erase them.

Deleting your account

If you delete your Zarok account, the connections and their data are deleted with it.

Data and privacy

Privacy policy

Access is encrypted

Access to services is stored on our server in encrypted form.

Nothing is sold

Connection data is not sold and not used for advertising.

You decide

Connecting is optional, and you can disconnect at any time.

Only what is needed

Task texts are not kept in the connection records. From Slack, Zarok takes only the message you pass to it yourself.

The names Google, Slack and Todoist belong to their owners and are used only to show what you can connect to. Each service works under its owner's rules.
