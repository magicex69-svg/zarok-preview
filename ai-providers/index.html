AI providers.

Who processes requests to Zarok AI.

Last updated October 25, 2026.

Main provider

Google — Gemini API, paid tier.

Requests are processed under Google's data processor terms. Google does not use them to train its models.

Processing locations are determined by Google.

Gemini API terms ↗

Backup provider

Moonshot AI PTE. LTD., Singapore.

Used only when the main provider is unavailable, and only for people who agreed to it in the app.

It may use requests to improve its models.

Not used in the EEA, the UK, Switzerland, Brazil and the Republic of Korea, when the country is unknown, or for requests in the Telegram bot chat.

What is sent

The text of the request, attached files and the part of your schedule needed for the answer.

Your account number, device IP address and payment details are not sent to AI providers.

If the list changes

When the list changes, we update this page.

If a new provider may train models on the data or is in another country, the app will ask for consent again.
