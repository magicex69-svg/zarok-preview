Deleting your account and data

The Zarok app · owner and responsible party: Zarok Studio

You can delete your user record in the app, with a request on this page or by email. Deleted data cannot be restored. Below is what is erased right away and what is kept for some time by law or to prevent abuse.

In the app: right away

If you signed in with Google or by email:

  1. Open the Profile tab.
  2. Tap your name at the top to open My profile.
  3. Under Account management, tap Delete account….
  4. Tick Delete account and confirm.

The account is deleted on all devices where you signed in to it.

Without an account: Profile → My profile → Data and sync → Reset data, tick Delete account and confirm. You can also erase individual sections there.

After deletion, the app keeps working on this device without sync to the server.

What is erased right away

  • The user record on the server: tasks, completion marks, progress, habits and courses.
  • The account: email address, password hash, the links to Google and Telegram.
  • Earlier versions of entries, the conversation with AI Zarok and the conversation with the developer.
  • Task images on our server.
  • Rewards, gifts, leaves, invitations, friends and counters stored in the user record.

What is kept, and for how long

  • Device and user record fingerprints: codes instead of the numbers themselves. They prevent receiving the same reward or gift twice. Device fingerprints that protect invitations stay in the shared list of devices for up to 400 days.
  • The cost accounting log: up to 3 years. It contains no name, email or text of entries.
  • The count of AI Zarok requests by Google Play purchase: a purchase fingerprint and the time of the last request. It is removed after 45 days without requests under that purchase.
  • The log of refund calculations: the date, the purchase fingerprint and the shares used, for up to 24 months. It contains no name, email or user record number.
  • Invitations. The other person in an invitation keeps a note without a name: the date and the outcome.
  • Reports. Reports about your user record are deleted, and your own reports lose their author. Reports about people in Friends are kept for 180 days, other reports for up to a year.
  • Technical server logs and error reports: 14 days.
  • Images in Telegram. Copies already sent to your chat with the Zarok bot stay in your chat. You can delete them in Telegram.
  • Purchases. Google Play keeps your purchase history under its own rules. Deleting the account does not cancel a subscription: cancel it in Google Play.

Without the app: a request on this page

If the app is already gone, send a request here.

The email you signed in with, or your Telegram username or ID.

What to delete

Request sent

Request number: .

We will reply within 30 days. To make sure the account is yours, we will write to the account email or ask you to confirm through Telegram. A confirmation of the request will arrive at your reply email.

Or write to support@zarok.app with the account email or the Telegram username you signed in with. If the account has an email address, write from that address.

We reply and delete the data within 30 days. You don't need to give your password: never write it in emails.

If you do nothing

A user record with no activity at all for a year is deleted automatically together with all its data, unless it has a paid period.

What stays with you

An export file, if you saved one, stays on your device. Entries that the app added to the phone calendar stay there until you delete them.
