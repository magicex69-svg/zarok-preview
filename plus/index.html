Subscription details, up front.

Price and free trial+

Purchases are made through Google Play. The price and currency depend on your account's region. A free trial is available only when the store offers one; the exact terms and the price after the trial are shown before you confirm.

Renewal and cancellation+

The subscription renews automatically until you cancel it. You can cancel in Google Play → Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions. Access lasts until the end of the paid or trial period. Deleting the app or your account does not cancel the subscription.

Access after changing phones+

Sign in to the same Zarok account. If access doesn't open, use “Restore purchases” in “AI Zarok & subscription.”

Is there a limit for AI Zarok?+

There is a daily limit and a limit per billing period. The trial allowance is lower. Current terms are shown in the app.

How is Zarok Max different?+

Zarok Max includes everything in Plus and 4 times more requests to AI Zarok. It comes as a monthly or yearly plan, with no free trial. From a monthly Plus plan, the switch happens right away: the full price is charged at the switch, and Google Play adds the unused part of Plus as extra days. You can return to Plus from the next charge. The price is shown in the app and in Google Play before purchase.

What is the family subscription?+

One person pays for Plus or Max and up to six people use it, each in their own account. Everyone has their own tasks and AI Zarok limit. Details are in the family subscription help.

Which widgets are free, and which are in Plus?+

Free: the main day and schedule widgets, “My Zarok,” “Next step,” “New task,” and the “Focus time” timer. In Plus: the AI Zarok widget, “Progress,” “At your pace,” “Day recap,” “Calendar” with its new views, “My steps and dates,” “Upcoming,” “Habits,” “Focus totals,” and “Moon,” plus filtering widget tasks by folder, category, course, or tag, palettes, and the minimal style. You can open a paid widget for a while with leaves: 1 leaf for 1 day. The AI Zarok widget can’t be opened with leaves.

What are the “Hanami,” “Tsukimi,” “Memes,” and “Memes · night” themes?+

Plus themes with their own scenes. In “Hanami,” Zarok marks a finished day with a DONE stamp, and “Tsukimi” is its night version. In “Memes,” Zarok answers your day with poses that nod to well-known memes, stickers sit nearby, and now and then a guest drops by. “Memes · night” is the dark version. You can also open a theme for a while with leaves: 1 leaf for 1 day.
