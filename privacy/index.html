Privacy Policy

Last updated October 25, 2026

The original text of this document is in English. Versions in other languages are translations made for your convenience. If you notice a discrepancy, please contact support@zarok.app.

In short

Zarok Studio is responsible for your data. Zarok uses your entries and settings to run your personal planner; they are kept on your device and, for sync, on our server in Germany. An account is optional. We do not sell data and do not use your entries for advertising, marketing or profiling. The free version of the Android app shows Google AdMob ads; subscribers see no ads. Each feature sends external services only the data it needs, as described below. AI Zarok sends requests to third-party AI services only after your separate consent, and connected AI apps receive only what you allow. You can export or delete your data, manage permissions and withdraw your consents. The sections below explain retention periods, your rights and how to contact us.

Who is responsible for your data

This policy describes what data the Zarok app processes on Google Play, on Huawei AppGallery and in Telegram: why it is needed and on what basis, where and for how long it is stored, which features use it, when data is passed to external services, which consents you give, how to withdraw them and what rights you have.

The app is developed by Zarok Studio. It decides why and how data is processed and is responsible for it: in terms of the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), it is the data controller. Contact us: support@zarok.app.

What Zarok is. It is a calendar and a planner for your personal routine. Supplement courses (dietary supplements and vitamins), meals, workouts and rituals in it are suggested types of entries: examples of what you can track, not recommendations. The app does not prescribe or advise anything: what to record and what to follow is for you to decide.

Words used in this policy. AI Zarok (also “the assistant”) is a feature of the app that turns your messages into suggested entries in your schedule and can read your schedule back to you; its answers are prepared by third-party artificial intelligence services (the “AI Zarok” section). Connected AI apps are external AI clients that you choose to connect to your plan (the “Connected AI apps” section). Zarok Plus and Max are the paid subscriptions. Your user record is the record the app keeps for you, with or without an account; an account is the optional sign-in by email or with Google.

Anyone may use the app; if you are under 13 (or under the age of digital consent in your country), use it with the permission of a parent or legal guardian. AI Zarok is available from age 16. Ads are shown only in the free version of the Android app (the “Ads” section).

What we collect and why

Your entries. Meals and what they contain, supplement courses with doses and phases, workouts and events, ritual steps, vows, check-offs, notes and links, including video links. This also includes task tags and priorities, saved search filters, the history of “Focus time” timer sessions and events from calendar files that you uploaded yourself. You enter this data yourself. It is needed to show your schedule, calculate progress and remind you about tasks.

Health information. We do not ask for health information, and the app has no separate fields for it. If you record such information yourself, for example supplement courses or how you feel in notes, you do so voluntarily. Such entries sync like all other entries and go to the assistant only if you write to it. Your onboarding answers about goals (sleep, body, stress) and supplements are kept only on the phone and are not sent to the server. By entering it, you give your explicit consent to this (Article 9 of the GDPR); you can withdraw it by deleting those entries. We do not draw conclusions about you from it and do not use it for ads. We advise you not to enter health information that your schedule does not need.

Settings. The name we use to address you, if you provided one, the theme and background pictures, the sections on the tab bar, status and category colors, reminders, the meal plan. They are stored together with your entries so that the app works the way you set it up. For tasks you have moved to another day, the app stores the number of moves; it syncs together with the other settings and is removed after 60 days. For “Year in review”, the app keeps a short summary for each year: how many times tasks were moved and on how many days each theme and Zarok look was chosen. It is stored together with the settings, for the last 5 years. The choice of welcome pictures (female or male version) is stored only on the device and is not sent to the server. A profile photo, if you chose one, is also stored only on the device.

Together. This section of the app is for keeping one task list with one other person or with your family: assigning tasks, checking off what is done and discussing them. If you attach a photo or a PDF to a task, the file, its name and its size are stored on the server with the task and are visible to all members of the list. The file is deleted together with the task; before that, the person who attached it or the list owner can delete it.

How shared lists are protected: on the server they are stored in encrypted form, and the key is kept separately from the data itself; between the app and the server, data travels only over a secure connection. Only the developer has access to the server.

How shared lists are deleted: a completed task, together with the comments on it, is erased 180 days after it was checked off; the activity feed is kept for 30 days. A task or a comment can be deleted right away, and it disappears for all members. When you leave a list or are removed from it, your tasks stay with the other members, but without your name. The owner can delete the whole list; if the owner deletes their account, the list is deleted for everyone.

AI Zarok

When. Only when you yourself send a request to the assistant; in the app, only after your consent. If you do not use the assistant, nothing is passed to AI services.

In the Telegram chat. If you connected Telegram, you can write to the assistant there too. Such messages are processed in the same way as requests in the app: they go to third-party AI services and are stored on our server on the same terms. The schedule changes only after your “Yes”. The bot chat has no consent screen and no age check: by sending a message to the assistant there, you use AI services on the terms of this section. Withdrawing consent in the app does not apply to the bot chat.

What data. The text of the request, including text recognized from voice; the content of an attached text file, for example a spreadsheet; an extract of the schedule needed for the answer: today's date, courses and habits with phases, doses, intake times and steps with their notes, today's meals with what they contain, events with times and days of the week, the dates of the meal plan, the names of folders and shared lists and, if you ask about tasks, the tasks for today and tomorrow. If the assistant asked a clarifying question, your answers go along with the request. The user record number, your device's IP address and payment information are not passed to AI services.

To whom. The main provider is Google LLC: our server sends requests to the paid Gemini API under Google's data processing terms, and under those terms Google does not use them to train its models. The current list of AI providers is on the zarok.app/ai-providers page. We have no AI model of our own. Data may be processed outside your country, including outside the European Union, for example in the United States, where the level of data protection may be lower. In the app, such a transfer takes place only with your explicit consent (the “Your consents and how to withdraw them” section); in the Telegram bot chat, when you write to the assistant yourself (above).

Backup provider. If Gemini does not respond, the same request may go to a backup provider, Moonshot AI PTE. LTD. (Singapore, the Kimi model). This provider may use requests to improve its models. The backup provider is covered by the current version of the AI consent: a line under the consent button says so and links to the “AI providers” page. It is not used in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, Switzerland, Brazil or the Republic of Korea, if you are under 16 or your country is unknown, or in the Telegram bot chat. The request contains no user record number, IP address or payment details.

Why. To compose an answer and suggest changes to the schedule. Changes are applied only after your confirmation.

Storage. A copy of the request and the answer is stored on our server for up to 180 days, to improve the assistant (see “Conversation with the assistant” in the “What we collect and why” section). The storage and use of the received data by AI services are governed by their terms.
