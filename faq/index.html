QUESTIONS & ANSWERS

The short version.
Subscription, account, AI Zarok.

Here you'll find common questions about Zarok. Subscription and cancellation are covered in the most detail: the store shows prices and terms before you pay.

What is “Zarok”?+

“Zarok” is a calendar for your day: tasks and events, courses, habits, food, steps, and reminders in one place. The app doesn't give medical advice or pick programs for you. You decide what goes into your schedule.

Who is the app for?+

For anyone who wants a clear daily routine: tasks, habits, and repeating steps in one calendar. The app is for all ages; under 13, with a parent’s permission. AI Zarok is available from age 16.

Do I need an account?+

No. You can use the app without an account. With an account, your data, Zarok, settings, and subscription are saved and move to a new phone. You can sign in with Google, with email and password, or with a one-time code from an email.

How do I move my data to a new phone?+

Sign in to the same account on your new phone. If your data or subscription didn't appear, open Profile and tap “Restore purchases” (Profile → name card → “AI Zarok & subscription”).

What's free?+

Day planning, calendar, courses, habits, food, statistics, video, reminders, the “Focus time” timer, the link to your phone calendar, data export, connecting AI apps (ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini), shared “Together” lists (no limit on the number of lists for ordinary use), and day and week summaries in the Telegram bot chat. Free widgets include the main day and schedule widgets, plus “My Zarok,” “Next step,” and “New task.”

What's included in “Zarok Plus”?+

AI Zarok and its widget, widgets with Zarok (“Progress,” “At your pace,” and “Day recap”), the “Calendar,” “My steps and dates,” “Upcoming,” “Habits,” “Focus totals,” and “Moon” widgets, filtering widget tasks by folder, category, course, or tag, glossy themes, the “Hanami,” “Tsukimi,” “Memes,” and “Memes · night” themes, widget palettes, and the minimal widget style. Also the observer role in the shared “Together” lists and the day layouts “Single day” and “Plan separately.” Paid widgets (except the AI Zarok widget) and Plus themes can be opened for a while with leaves: you get them for watching ads in the free version, and 1 leaf opens a widget or a theme for 1 day. AI Zarok can’t be opened with leaves.

Which widgets are free, and which are in Plus?+

Free: the main day and schedule widgets, “My Zarok,” “Next step,” “New task,” and the “Focus time” timer. In Plus: the AI Zarok widget, “Progress,” “At your pace,” “Day recap,” “Calendar” with its new views, “My steps and dates,” “Upcoming,” “Habits,” “Focus totals,” and “Moon,” plus filtering widget tasks by folder, category, course, or tag, palettes, and the minimal style. You can open a paid widget for a while with leaves: 1 leaf for 1 day. The AI Zarok widget can’t be opened with leaves.

How do I subscribe?+

You subscribe in the app through Google Play, monthly or yearly. The price is shown in the payment window before you confirm your purchase. A free trial is only available when the store offers one; its length and the price after the trial are shown before you pay.

How much does the subscription cost?+

The price depends on the country of your Google Play account. The store and the payment window show it before you confirm the purchase.

How do I cancel my subscription?+

At any time: Google Play → profile menu → “Payments & subscriptions” → “Subscriptions” → “Zarok” → “Cancel subscription”. Canceling stops future charges, and access stays until the end of the paid period. If you cancel during the free trial, you aren't charged, and access stays until the trial ends.

Will I be charged if I forget to cancel the free trial?+

Yes. Unless you cancel at least 24 hours before the free trial ends, it automatically converts to a paid subscription, and Google Play will charge you for the first paid period.

What happens if a payment fails?+

Google Play retries the charge for several days, and AI Zarok stays available during that time. If the payment still doesn't go through, the subscription is paused until you update your payment method in Google Play. You can see your subscription status in your profile: Profile → name card → “AI Zarok & subscription”.

What happens if the subscription price changes?+

Google Play will let you know in advance. The new price applies only from the next paid period. If your consent to the new price is required, the subscription won't renew at the new price without it.

Will I get a refund if I change my mind?+

No: payments are final and non-refundable, except where the law requires a refund. After you cancel, you keep access until the end of the paid period, and unused time is not refunded. Only Google refunds a Google Play purchase, under its own rules: “Payments & subscriptions” → “Budget & history” → the “Zarok” purchase → “Request a refund”. In the European Union, the European Economic Area and the United Kingdom, you have a 14-day right to withdraw, unless you have lost it under the law. See the Terms of Use, “Refunds” section, for details.

Does the subscription work on another phone?+

Yes. The subscription is tied to your Zarok account. Sign in to the same account on the other device.

How does AI Zarok work?+

AI Zarok records courses, meals, workouts, and ritual steps, and can summarize your schedule. Before anything is applied, AI Zarok shows what it understood and waits for your confirmation. AI Zarok doesn't give advice on nutrition, supplements, workouts, or health.

Why does AI Zarok sometimes make mistakes?+

Answers from AI Zarok are generated by a third-party AI service. We don't have our own language model. AI Zarok can make mistakes when reading text, so check the suggested entries.

Who can use AI Zarok?+

From age 16. Under 18, with permission from a parent or legal guardian. Before you first use AI Zarok, you confirm your age once and agree to requests being processed by a third-party AI service.

Why does AI Zarok have a daily limit?+

The limit protects the service from overload. Besides the daily limit, there is a limit for the period. The trial limit is listed on the subscription screen; when few requests are left, the remaining number is shown in the AI Zarok chat.

How do I delete my data or account?+

You can erase your data or delete your account right in the app: Profile → “Account” → “Manage account”. You can also submit a deletion request on the account deletion page.

How do reminders work?+

By default, “Zarok” reminds you to come back to your day on its own. A reminder for a specific task is turned on separately, using the checkbox next to that task. Allow notifications for the app in your phone's settings.

Which languages does Zarok work in?+

The app works in English, Russian, and other languages. It follows your phone language. To pick another one, open Profile → “App settings” → “Language” in the app. The website is in English and Russian.

Does “Zarok” work in a browser?+

No. “Zarok” opens in Telegram and on Android.

What can the calendar and planning do?+

The calendar (month, week, year, and day by the hour), search and filters, tags and importance, “Focus time” (a timer), “Day presets,” and meetings from a calendar file next to your plan.
