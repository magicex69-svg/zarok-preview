The day becomes clearer.

Zarok brings your plans into one schedule: today's tasks, habits, and courses.

Your whole day in order

Tasks, events, and rituals line up by time. Check them off in one tap.

  1. Day presets

    Your own set of tasks for different kinds of days, like weekdays and weekends.

  2. From any app

    Tap Share in your browser, mail, or messenger, and the text becomes a task.

  3. From the app icon and quick settings

    On Android, long-press the Zarok icon or open quick settings to add a task in a second.

From a year to an hour

Switch between year, month, week, and an hour-by-hour day.

  1. Search and filters

    Find an entry by keyword and keep only what you need on screen.

  2. Meetings from a calendar file

    Open an .ics calendar file, and its meetings appear next to your plan.

  3. Your phone calendar

    Show phone events in one schedule. You choose which calendars appear.

The right step on the right day

Turn a big goal, like studying or training, into daily steps.

  1. Course stages

    Split a course into stages, and Zarok shows which one is on now.

  2. Active periods and breaks

    Set when you practice and when you rest.

  3. Habits

    Pick the days and time, and Zarok reminds you.

Your plan on the home screen

See and check off tasks without opening the app. On Android.

  1. Free

    Day plan, schedule, next step, and timer on your home screen.

  2. More with Plus

    Progress, calendar, habits, and task filters by folder or tag.

  3. Matches your theme

    Widgets follow your theme colors.

One task at a time

A Focus time timer for 15, 25, or 45 minutes, or your own time.

  1. The timer is always at hand

    On Android, it runs in a notification, on a widget, and in quick settings, even with the app closed.

  2. Tags and importance

    Mark entries so what matters stays in sight.

  3. Filter by tag

    Keep only entries with the tag you need on screen.

Your rhythm in numbers

Stats build up from your check-offs.

  1. Closed days and streaks

    A closed day means every task is checked off. See how many you have in a row.

  2. Time by category

    How much time goes to each category.

  3. Month ring

    The closed days of the month come together in one ring.

Your tasks right in chats

The bot @day_calendar_bot runs “Together” lists in groups and opens the app inside Telegram.

  1. Day and week summary

    Your plan for today and the week right in the chat.

  2. A task in one line

    In any chat, type @day_calendar_bot and a task, and it lands in your plan.

  3. Stickers and emoji

    Zarok sets for your chats.

Everything the bot can do

Together with friends.

Friends by @username, shared streaks, and a week-long race. Your tasks and notes stay private.

Streaks and rewards.

Every closed day continues your streak, and rewards mark your path.

You can share your streak and weekly summary as a picture: it shows only numbers, no task names.

App icons

On Android, the pinned Zarok shortcut with Topa’s face changes with your day: a streak, a reward, the evening, a holiday. The Topa, Memes, and Sakura sets come with Plus; the Classic icon is open to everyone.

Warm look

Plus themes come with their own scenes

“Hanami”: the DONE stamp

Cherry blossoms, lanterns, and petals. When every task of the day is checked off, Zarok sets a DONE stamp. “Tsukimi” is the night version of the theme.

“Memes”: a finished day

When the day is closed, Zarok greets you with a speech bubble and confetti.

Meme poses and stickers

Zarok answers your day with poses that nod to well-known memes. Stickers sit nearby: a crown, a flame, a chart going up.

Guests

Now and then a capybara, a cat, a frog, or a goose drops by. Never two days in a row, so every visit is a surprise.

The themes are part of Plus. You can also open a theme for a while with leaves.
