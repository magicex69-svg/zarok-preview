FEATURES

The day becomes
clearer.

Zarok turns your plans into a schedule: from today's tasks to repeating habits and courses.

Your day on one screen

Tasks, events, and rituals go in time order. Check off what's done and see how much you've finished.

  • Tasks and events in time order
  • One-tap check-off
  • Day presets for different kinds of days

Calendar by month, week, and year

Switch between views, from the whole year down to the day by the hour.

  • Month, week, year, and day by the hour
  • Search and filters for entries
  • Meetings from a calendar file next to your plan
  • Link to your phone calendar, with your permission

Courses & habits

Set stages, active periods, and breaks. The app shows the right step on the right day.

  • Course stages and their order
  • Active periods and breaks
  • Habits with repeats and reminders

Widgets on your home screen

See your plan and check off tasks without opening the app. Widgets are on Android.

  • Free: “My Zarok,” “Next step,” “New task,” and the main day widgets
  • In Plus: “Progress,” “Calendar” with new views, “Upcoming,” “Habits,” “Focus totals,” “Moon,” and more
  • Filtering widget tasks by folder, category, course, or tag is in Plus
  • With the “Hanami” and “Memes” themes, widgets take on their look
  • You can open a paid widget for a while with leaves

Focus time & tags

A timer for one task, plus order in your entries: tags and importance help you keep track of what matters.

  • “Focus time” is a timer for one task
  • Tags and importance for entries
  • Filters to find what you need fast

Progress & statistics

Closed days, repeats, and time by category help you see your rhythm in numbers.

  • Closed days and repeat streaks
  • Time by category
  • A ring showing closed days for the month

Zarok in Telegram

The bot @day_calendar_bot shows your plan, runs “Together” lists in groups, and opens the app right inside Telegram.

  • Day and week summaries, free for everyone
  • A bot menu with buttons
  • A task in one line from any chat: “@day_calendar_bot task tomorrow 10:00”
  • Zarok stickers and emoji
THEMES & SCENES / PLUS

Themes with their own scenes

“Hanami,” “Tsukimi,” “Memes,” and “Memes · night” change more than colors: each theme has its own pictures and its own Zarok.

“Hanami”: the DONE stamp

Cherry blossoms, lanterns, and petals. When every task of the day is checked off, Zarok sets a DONE stamp. “Tsukimi” is the night version of the theme.

“Memes”: a finished day

A finished day brings Zarok with a speech bubble and confetti.

Meme poses and stickers

Zarok answers your day with poses that nod to well-known memes. Stickers sit nearby: a crown, a flame, a chart going up.

Guests

Now and then a capybara, a cat, a frog, or a goose drops by. Never two days in a row, so there is always a surprise.

“Memes · night” is the dark version of the meme theme. With the “Hanami” and “Memes” themes, home-screen widgets change too: petals and soft light, or bright sticker-style check marks.

The themes are part of Plus. You can also open a theme for a while with leaves.
